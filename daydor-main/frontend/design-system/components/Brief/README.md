# Brief

The top of the founder's home: a greeting, one sentence of what happened and what needs them, and three or four counts.

**Consumer provides:** `greeting`, optional `date`, `summary` (a sentence or two; wrap the call to action in `<strong>`), `stats` (`{value, label, tone?: 'warning'|'brand'}`), `children`.

- The summary is written by Daydor and follows the content rules: outcome first, numbers exact, the single most urgent deadline named.
- Stats: "Need you" (warning) always first; then "Running now" (brand); then done and upcoming. No more than four.
- Collapse the brief once the person scrolls into the conversation; it's a morning glance, not a dashboard.
